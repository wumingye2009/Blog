The Seven-Layer Architecture of AI Systems

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is not a single program, but a **multi-layered ecosystem**. To better understand how modern AI systems function, we can view them through a **seven-layer architecture model**, which connects data, models, platforms, orchestration, inference, integration, and applications.

Conclusion

The seven-layer AI system architecture provides a structured perspective:

  • Layers 1–3 form the foundation (data → preprocessing → models).
  • Layers 4–6 enable orchestration, inference, and integration.
  • Layer 7 delivers applications to end-users.

By separating AI into these seven layers, we can clearly see both the technical stack and the application potential, making it easier for researchers, developers, and businesses to navigate this rapidly evolving field.
