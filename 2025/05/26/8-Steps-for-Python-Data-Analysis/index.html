Python Data Analysis in 8 Steps: From Zero to Insight

Have you ever stared at a messy dataset and thought:

“Where should I even start?”

Don’t worry — with Python, data analysis can actually feel like an 8-step adventure.

Think of it as a journey: from loading your dataset, exploring its secrets, to finally unlocking insights.

Grab your coffee ☕ and let’s dive in!

🧹 Step 5: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

This is where you start asking questions and summarizing patterns.

EDA helps you discover:

  • Central tendencies (mean, median)
  • Group-level insights (e.g., salary by department)
  • Relationships between variables (correlations, trends)

📝 Step 8: Model Training & Evaluation

Finally, use your cleaned and engineered dataset to train and test models.
